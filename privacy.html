TASC Connect: privacy

Visit tracking for Central Bank of India, Regional Office Udaipur

Who uses TASC Connect

TASC Connect is an internal tool for staff of Regional Office Udaipur and its branches, to record visits made for the bank.

What Google sign-in shares

When you sign in with Google, TASC Connect receives only your name and email address. It does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What TASC Connect stores

  • Your staff profile from the Regional Office staff list (name, employee ID, designation, branch, phone, date of birth). Your date of birth is used only to confirm who you are the first time you sign in.
  • The Gmail addresses linked to your profile, and when they were last used.
  • Visit records you enter: date and time, location, people met, business details, remarks and photos.

Who can see it

  • You see your own profile and your own visits.
  • Your branch head sees your branch's visits, in order to verify them.
  • The Regional Office admin and the viewers they appoint see all visits.
  • Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than tracking the bank's visits.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Supabase in its Mumbai (India) region, with access rules on every record. Photos are stored privately and open only through short-lived links.

Removing your access

You can remove Gmails linked to your profile from Profile in the app. For anything else, contact the Regional Office, Udaipur.
